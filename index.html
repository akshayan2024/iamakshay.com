agentic AI app6 days to a working app

VoiceInPublic™

problem
Creators know they should post on LinkedIn, but finding something worth saying, in their own voice, eats hours.
built
A team of AI agents: a trend scout reads Reddit, news, RSS and Hacker News, a strategist picks angles, and a writer drafts posts that are checked for facts and voice. Nothing goes out until the creator approves that exact post.
result
Trademark filed, landing page built, and an invite-only beta with 5 testers is being set up.
AI + dataabout 2 weeks

Signal Desk

problem
Solo YouTube creators track competitors and trends by hand, across dozens of tabs.
built
A research desk that pulls channels, transcripts and comments into one database, makes them searchable by meaning, and uses AI for daily briefs, content gaps and idea generation.
result
A 10-tab workspace for one creator. Data foundation first, AI on top: the same pattern I used in enterprise.
chrome extension1 day

YouScript

problem
Getting a clean transcript out of a YouTube video, or a Short, takes far too many clicks.
built
A Chrome extension that adds a Copy transcript button. It falls back to the caption track when YouTube's panel isn't there, and runs with no server and no tracking.
result
Built, tested and packaged for the Chrome Web Store, with listing and privacy policy, in a single day.
iOS app1 day

FeedCut

problem
Instagram and YouTube are useful until Reels, Shorts and endless feeds swallow the evening.
built
An iPhone app that opens Instagram and YouTube with the addictive parts stripped out before they ever appear. You choose filters like "block Reels", and it shows how much time you spend on each platform, stored only on your phone.
result
A working SwiftUI app with switchable filters and a daily usage dashboard.
iOS app1 day

Solace

problem
Focus and sleep sounds are the same loop every time, whatever you're actually doing.
built
An iPhone app that generates music live from your day: the weather, the time, your walking pace and your Apple Watch heart rate. A horizon scene moves with it, and it rains on screen when it rains outside.
result
A real-time sound engine written from scratch, where every input can be switched off and location is rounded to about 1 km.
